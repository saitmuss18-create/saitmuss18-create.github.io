Privacy Policy — YT Analytics Local

Last updated: October 21, 2026

YT Analytics Local ("the app") is an internal tool used only by its owner to manage his own YouTube channels. This policy explains how the app handles Google user data.

Data the app accesses

After the owner signs in with Google OAuth 2.0, the app accesses, through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, only the owner's own channel data: channel and video information, video uploads and metadata, thumbnails, comments on the owner's videos, and channel analytics reports.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and update the owner's videos and to show the owner reports about his own channels. The app does not use the data for advertising and does not build profiles of anyone.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens and reports are stored only on the owner's computer. The app has no servers. Google user data is not sold, transferred or shared with any third party.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also complies with the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored tokens and reports are deleted by the owner on request or when the tool is no longer used.
